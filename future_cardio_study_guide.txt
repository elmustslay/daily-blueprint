FUTURE CARDIO STUDY & NUTRITION GUIDE
(For Implementation in 2 Months when Standing Desk & Walking Pad with Incline are acquired)

================================================================================
1. THE CORE OBJECTIVE: ZONE 2 COGNITIVE ENHANCEMENT
================================================================================
You should acquire a motorized walking pad with incline capability for your home study/reading desk to keep your Heart Rate (HR) strictly between 120 and 140 bpm. Ensure your Apple Watch is configured with vibration alerts to notify you when you drift out of this range.

Why this range?
* Below 120 bpm: The stimulation is too low to elevate blood flow and BDNF (brain growth factors) significantly.
* Above 140 bpm: Your body shifts from aerobic (Zone 2) to anaerobic (Zone 3/4) energy production. Lactate begins to accumulate, breathing gets heavy, and physical exertion distracts your brain, making it difficult to read and comprehend text.
* The 120–140 bpm sweet spot: Maximizes cerebral oxygenation and brain plasticity while keeping your body quiet enough to absorb complex information.
* Study Type Compatibility: Strictly limit walking to passive or semi-passive study blocks (reading textbooks/papers, reviewing flashcards, watching educational videos). Avoid walking during active coding, typing, or heavy writing sessions due to screen bounce and hand instability.

================================================================================
2. EXTRA WEEKLY ENERGY EXPENDITURE (WALKING SCHEDULE)
================================================================================
Walking at 120–140 bpm burns approximately 250 extra calories (kcal) per hour. You will only walk during passive/semi-passive study blocks. 

To prevent sleep disruption (elevated core body temp close to bed) and overtraining, there is no walking during weekday evening study blocks. Walking is strictly limited to Saturday afternoon:

* Monday:    No walking                               ->  0 kcal
* Tuesday:   No walking                               ->  0 kcal
* Wednesday: No walking                               ->  0 kcal
* Thursday:  No walking                               ->  0 kcal
* Friday:    No walking                               ->  0 kcal
* Saturday:  1.5 hours (afternoon study block only)*    ->  ~375 kcal extra
  * Walk only during the Afternoon Study block (2:00–3:30 pm).
  * Do not walk during the Morning Study or Morning Read.
* Sunday:    No walking                               ->  0 kcal

TOTAL EXTRA WEEKLY BURN: ~375 kcal

================================================================================
3. NUTRITIONAL COMPENSATION: THE ~56% REPLACEMENT RULE
================================================================================
To accelerate fat loss without crashing your metabolism or dropping below your 1,724 kcal/day RMR, you should replace approximately 56% of the new calories you burn.

* Total Weekly Burn: ~375 kcal
* Target Weekly Replacement (56%): ~210 kcal
* Unreplaced Deficit (Faster Fat Loss): ~165 kcal/week

================================================================================
4. REALISTIC FOOD REPLACEMENT PLAN (EASY PREP)
================================================================================
There will be no changes to your meal-prepped quinoa/pasta or Chipotle orders. Compensation is done via a pre-workout banana boost:

* +2 Bananas/week (on Mon/Thu pre-workout) = +210 kcal
* Total: 210 kcal (56% replacement)

Timing Tip: To avoid stomach heaviness on lifting days, eat the first banana ~45 mins before and the second banana ~15 mins before your workout.
