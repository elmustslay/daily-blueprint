NIGHTTIME SLEEP ONSET PROTOCOL (THE MICRO-DOSE CARB FIX)

BACKGROUND & REASONING:
When deep in the "200-Day Grind" caloric deficit, the body experiences energy deprivation. This causes the adrenal glands to pump out cortisol and adrenaline at night, putting the body in a "fight or flight" mode. This manifests as feeling "wired and tired" and running "too warm" to sleep, because the stress hormones increase heart rate and metabolic heat production, preventing the core body temperature from dropping (which is necessary for sleep onset).

THE SOLUTION:
A micro-dose of low-GI carbohydrates (~10-15g) is needed to cause a small insulin spike. This insulin release blunts the evening cortisol, allows tryptophan to cross the blood-brain barrier to produce serotonin/melatonin, and helps the body power down without causing a secondary hypoglycemic crash later in the night.

THE PRIMARY OPTION (TRADER JOE'S KIWI):
- Item: 1 or 2 Fresh Kiwis.
- Why: Kiwis are scientifically proven to aid sleep onset. They contain high concentrations of naturally occurring serotonin, a massive antioxidant payload to combat systemic deficit inflammation, and are perfectly low on the Glycemic Index.

THE LOGISTICS / TIMING (THE "IMPOSSIBLE TRILEMMA" FIX):
Eating the kiwi right before bed creates three biological conflicts:
1. You can't brush immediately after eating the kiwi (the acidity softens enamel, and brushing strips it away).
2. You can't brush after washing your face in the shower (toothpaste residue causes perioral dermatitis / breakouts around the mouth).
3. You can't just *not* brush your teeth after eating the kiwi (sugar left on teeth overnight causes cavities).

The Solution (The 30-Minute Pre-Shower Buffer):
1. T-Minus 120 Minutes (The Insulin Trigger): Exactly 30 minutes before your Bed Prep routine begins (during your final Study or Chores block), eat exactly 1 Kiwi. Immediately swish your mouth vigorously with water to wash away the sugar and neutralize the acidity.
2. The Chemical Cascade: Your blood sugar peaks. Insulin sweeps amino acids out of the blood, clearing the way for Tryptophan to enter the brain and begin converting to Serotonin. Meanwhile, your enamel has a full 30 minutes to re-harden.
3. T-Minus 90 Minutes (The Bed Prep / Shower): Your Bed Prep begins. Because 30 minutes have passed, it is now safe to brush your teeth FIRST. Then, do your cleansing oil, get in the shower, and wash your face. Your face remains clean from toothpaste, and your enamel is protected.
4. T-Minus 30 Minutes: You finish your Night Supplements (Magnesium + Finasteride). Serotonin is now converting heavily to Melatonin right as your core body temperature crashes from the hot shower.
5. T-Minus 0 Minutes (Bed): Cortisol is blunted, Melatonin is surging, and core body temperature is bottomed out. You pass out immediately.

BACKUP OPTIONS (IF TJ'S DOESN'T HAVE FRESH KIWIS):
1. Half a Banana: 
   - Pros: Zero friction (already bought for pre-workout), high in natural muscle relaxants (Potassium & Magnesium).
   - Cons: Slightly higher Glycemic Index, so slightly faster/sharper insulin spike.
   - Prep: Wrap the other half in foil/plastic wrap and store in the fridge. Note: The peel WILL turn black due to cold oxidation, but the fruit inside remains perfectly fresh.
2. Half a Green Apple:
   - Pros: Low GI, keeps forever in the fridge, very slow and steady insulin release.
   - Cons: No serotonin/antioxidant payload like kiwis or muscle relaxants like bananas.
