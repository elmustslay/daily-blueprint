FUTURE SKINCARE PLAN:
1. Move Joseon Alpha-Arbutin to EVERY morning (including no sunscreen days): Ascorbyl 12% -> wait 90s -> Joseon -> wait 2m -> Moisturizer (CeraVe PM) -> sunscreen (on work days).
2. On Sundays, bring back Dermaroller: Skip Joseon, use ONLY Moisturizer (CeraVe PM) (wait 30m after rolling to avoid flushing/hyperpigmentation).
3. Introduce minoxidil for night (except weekends and Mondays) and in-shower lotion for morning.
4. CeravePm might be replaced by Vanicream if it's irritating after a few weeks.
5. Switch from reusable microfiber facial washcloths to buying/using disposable face towels (e.g., Clean Skin Club Clean Towels XL) to dry face post-wash to eliminate bacterial transfer.

FUTURE LAUNDRY PLAN:
1. Purchase 6 Supima cotton pillowcases and a set of percale sheets/pillowcases (giving a total of 8 pillowcases and 1 bedsheet in rotation).
2. Swap pillowcases every night for optimal skin hygiene.
3. Wash bedding/pillowcases separately from heavy towels to prevent friction, pilling, and lint transfer, and dry them on LOW heat.
4. If you ever decide to stop using small washcloths for your face and start using your large bath towels for both body AND face:
   a. You MUST wash them on a Quick Cycle and have them be single use to prevent acne/bacteria from damp towels sitting around.
   b. Since you have 4 towels, this will wear the towels out in about a year (one towel handles 100 wash), requiring you to buy a new $30-$40 set yearly (a worthwhile trade-off for clear skin and convenience).

FUTURE NUTRITION PLAN:
1. Replace pre-workout Banana with Highly Branched Cyclic Dextrin (HBCD) for optimal gym performance.
   - Why: HBCD requires practically zero digestion and has extremely low osmolarity. It empties from the stomach instantly, meaning 100% of blood flow stays in your working muscles (delivering a better pump and zero nausea). It also provides a direct, sustained drip of pure glucose without an insulin crash, unlike fructose-containing bananas which must be processed by the liver first.
   - How: Mix ~25g of HBCD (the carb equivalent of one banana) directly into your regular Hydro Flask (it dissolves easily and completely clear). Start sipping it 15 minutes before your first working set and finish it throughout the first 30 minutes of the workout as an Intra-workout fuel.
