TIGRINYA 102-LEVEL FEATURES — NOT YET IMPLEMENTED
===================================================
Target: Tigray Tigrinya (Ethiopian variety)

These features are needed to go beyond 101 (CEFR A2) and reach
full 102 (CEFR B1 / Intermediate-Low). Add these after completing
the current curriculum and verifying foundational mastery.

1. RELATIVE CLAUSES
   "The man who came" → ዝመጸ ሰብ
   "The food that I ate" → ዝበላዕኩዎ መግቢ
   Relative marker: ዝ- (zi-) prefix on verbs
   This is one of the biggest structural differences from Amharic
   (which uses የ- and -ው suffix patterns).

2. CONDITIONAL SENTENCES
   "If you go" → እንተ ከይድካ
   "If he comes" → እንተ መጺኡ
   Conditional marker: እንተ (ente)
   Also: "If...then" → እንተ...ድማ

3. PASSIVE VOICE BASICS
   "The book was read" → እቲ መጽሐፍ ተነቢቡ
   Passive prefix: ተ- (te-)
   Many verbs already use ተ- in their base form (e.g. ተዛረብ),
   so this requires teaching the distinction.

4. MORE COMPLEX NEGATION
   Past negative: ኣይ + past stem + ን
   Future negative: ኣይክ- + stem + ን + እዩ
   Negative imperative: ኣይት- + stem (Don't do X!)

5. ADVANCED VERB FORMS
   - Causative: ኣ- prefix (ኣበልዐ = "He fed / made eat")
   - Reciprocal: ተ- prefix with plural (ተፋቲዎም = "They loved each other")
   - Frequentative/Intensive: Gemination patterns

6. SUBORDINATE CLAUSES
   "Because I went" → ስለ ዝኸድኩ
   "When he comes" → ምስ ዝመጽእ
   "Before I eat" → ቅድሚ ዝበልዕ
   Uses ዝ- relative marker + preposition

7. COMPARATIVE & SUPERLATIVE
   "Bigger than" → ካብ... ዝዓበየ
   "The biggest" → እቲ ዝዓበየ

8. REPORTED SPEECH
   "He said that..." → ...ኢሉ ተዛሪቡ
   "She asked if..." → ...ድያ ኢላ ሓቲታ

9. EXPANDED VOCABULARY (~500+ words for B1)
   - Professions (25+ terms)
   - Weather vocabulary
   - Health/medical terms
   - Shopping/commerce expanded
   - Technology terms
   - Abstract concepts (justice, freedom, truth)

10. DISCOURSE MARKERS & FILLERS
    - ማለት (that is to say)
    - ብርግጽ (certainly)
    - ብሓቂ (really/truly)
    - ግን ድማ (however/but then)

11. THE CONTINUOUS PREFIX ("WHILE")
    - "While eating" → ናበልዐ (nabel'a) or እናበልዐ (enabel'a)
    - "While dancing" → ናወዝወዘ (nawezweze)
    - Continuous marker: ና- (na-) or እና- (ena-) prefixed to verbs.
    - Used frequently in storytelling and conversational Tigrinya.
